BenefitPrism

See your health benefits more clearly.

BenefitPrism is a personal health benefits project designed to make claims, explanations of benefits, healthcare costs, and out-of-pocket responsibility easier to understand.

About BenefitPrism

Health plan information can be difficult to connect across care, claims, coverage, payments, and member responsibility. BenefitPrism explores ways to bring that information together into a clearer, more understandable view.

Privacy

BenefitPrism accesses health plan information only with the member's authorization. Health information is not sold or used for advertising.

BenefitPrism is currently under development and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any health plan or healthcare provider.
